## Active focus

**Mountain-range morphology and ownership.** Human review of the seed-12345
matched relief packet finds that mountain systems often read as long,
flat-topped plateaus—“a giant long Uluru”—rather than internally organized
ranges. Relief exaggeration makes them usable and visually impressive, but also
helped conceal the modeled plateau morphology.

The completed discriminating packet compares the same three range targets through:

1. coarse tectonic envelope;
2. fine interpolated/structured base;
3. pre-erosion hydrologically integrated surface;
4. raw eroded surface before final drainage integration;
5. final post-integration surface;
6. tectonic thickening and legacy repeated-uplift source;
7. slope/hillshade without snow-color saturation;
8. drainage/divides and integration cuts.

### Discriminating result

- The plateau envelope is already present in coarse elevation and follows the
  smooth, capped tectonic-thickening distance bands.
- Fine coarse interpolation and `FineBase::base_elevation` produce
  byte-identical matched images at all three range cameras. The default fine
  structural layer adds exactly no range organization.
- Pre-erosion hydrology changes only sparse outlet paths; it does not own the
  plateau.
- Erosion creates much of the visible marginal/ridge relief, but carves a
  pre-existing tableland rather than replacing it with a crest hierarchy.
- Final drainage integration is visually secondary to range morphology.
- With repeated legacy uplift disabled, the long tabletop/ring grammar remains.
  Re-enabling it raises and snow-saturates the same envelope, preserves broad
  interfluves and changes basin/lake outcomes. Hold-and-carve is an amplifier
  and coupling risk, not the original grammar owner.

The ownership order is therefore:

1. **Primary:** smooth/capped coarse distance-band orogen construction;
2. **Primary missing mechanism:** no fine crest/divide/ridge hierarchy;
3. **Secondary amplifier:** repeated broad legacy uplift during erosion;
4. **Not primary:** hydrologic integration or relief presentation.

The next architecture comparison should preserve height/volume budgets while
testing organized range form: first a segment-varying boundary source against
the current smooth distance band, then a minimal causal crest/divide skeleton
inside the same broad envelope. Do not tune uplift gain or add undirected noise.

## Settled project-level conclusions

- Hex3 is an authentic systemic world generator using reduced causal models,
  semantic geographic objects and explicit cartographic presentation.
- Physical state, semantic interpretation and presentation are separate owners.
- True-scale Physical relief is useful for truth/diagnosis but visually flat at
  planetary and broad regional scales.
- Authentic (~25×) and Dramatic (~51×) relief are both fit-for-purpose
  cartographic presentations; their exaggeration is not itself a model defect.
- Climate and hydrology are relatively cheap, reusable foundations with high
  option value for future ecology, ice, soils, resources, settlement and other
  stages. Correspondence tests define their claims and guide selective repair;
  they are not presumptive deletion tests.
- Missing systems compete with repairing, simplifying or deleting existing
  systems. No ecology/sediment/civilization sequence is automatic.

## Findings that must remain visible

### Mountain grammar and tectonic terrain

- The accepted legacy path is present-boundary response terrain, not a
  geological reconstruction. It computes tectonic history/work that legacy
  elevation does not consume.
- Arc/collision response is used first as static coarse tectonic thickness and
  again as a repeated source over 200 dimensionless erosion steps. Treat this as
  a calibrated **hold-and-carve servo**, not shared-clock uplift and erosion.
- Erosion is a major morphology owner: seed 12345 gains substantial
  25–100-km relief and the highest selected range's fixed-mask peak rises from
  6.01 to 9.03 km.
- Significant ranges include very large/broad summit areas and low-grade caps.
  Human review confirms this is visible modeled morphology, not only a metric.
- The likely conceptual defect is that an orogen is represented mainly as a
  scalar uplift footprint. A convincing range needs an internal crest, pass,
  subsidiary-ridge, valley and divide hierarchy.
- Range ancestry has now confirmed this: the fine interpolant and fine base are
  visually byte-identical, and the binary no-uplift control retains the same
  broad tabletop grammar.
- Do not respond with undirected noise or relief-scale tuning. Candidate
  replacements include skeleton-organized uplift, drainage-organized range
  construction, localized/evolving forcing, or a simpler disclosed authentic
  range generator.
- The non-legacy orogen ladder remains quarantined. It should not become the
  answer merely because the legacy morphology is weak.

### Lakes and basin storage

- Seed 12345 lake area changes materially with fine budget: 4.71% of land at
  the 250k probe versus 3.30% at the 1M corpus.
- The 250k surface contains continent-scale lakes: 2.60M, 1.20M and 1.04M km².
  Catchment/lake ratios are generally low and some lake surfaces are high in the
  land hypsometry.
- Human/technical relief inspection finds the largest lake conspicuously round
  and several large/high lakes expose radial or triangulated basin geometry.
- The lake climate-ratio dial changes water levels without recomputing
  precipitation, rivers or erosion, so “wetter/drier climate” is presently
  split across systems.
- Drainage integration does not directly cut the selected lake cells, but
  catchments, outlets, storage priors and resolution survival remain open.
- Needed discrimination: matched basin topology/outlet/repair views and
  coarse/fine object ancestry—not threshold or abundance tuning.

### Rivers, drainage and semantic importance

- “Major river” currently has several conflicting meanings. For seed 12345:
  the highest-discharge selected river is only 244 km; the longest trunk is
  5,278 km but order 2 with modest wet-area supply; the highest-order target is
  475 km and order 4.
- The longest trunk is barely communicated by the current Major presentation.
  Semantic selection and presentation selection therefore disagree.
- River hierarchy is weak/fragmented in the 250k audit: maximum order 4,
  bifurcation ratio 8.1, low drainage density and some implausible long-profile
  shapes.
- Do not tune a visibility threshold first. Define separate authoritative
  concepts for catchment/discharge importance, network hierarchy, trunk length
  and cartographic selection.
- Retain the area-aware drainage graph and semantic separation; repair object
  identity, hierarchy and presentation contracts selectively.

### Drainage-integration repair

- Hydrology deliberately carves outlet paths and the final surface adopts those
  cuts. This is an authentic geological-integration hack, not erosion.
- Sparse exact provenance is now retained. At seed 12345/250k, integration
  changes 1,005 cells with a maximum cut of about 195 m.
- The longest selected trunk contains 64 cut cells; the largest range contains
  195. Some apparent water gaps or transcontinental coherence may therefore be
  authored by repair.
- The hack is not rejected. Its provenance, object consequences and cheaper
  alternatives must be visible in diagnostics.

### Climate and atmosphere

- The coarse climate has real reduced mechanisms: prescribed circulation,
  projected terrain-modified wind and finite-volume moisture transport.
- Its precipitation is normalized relative climatology, not a closed/calibrated
  water budget; fine climate is mostly transferred from coarse state in the
  product configuration.
- Atmospheric `uplift` is an ascent/rainout index and is easily confused with
  tectonic/erosional uplift.
- Climate and hydrology are cheap relative to erosion and are important future
  inputs. A simple-climatology comparison asks what additional conditional
  geography consumers may rely on; it does not assume removal is desirable.
- Open tests: wind reversal/rain-shadow response, shared ocean identification,
  moisture budget/normalization meaning, precipitation's effect on river
  importance, and downstream stability.

### Presentation and visual evidence

- Physical relief is expectedly flat; it remains necessary diagnostic evidence.
- Authentic and Dramatic both satisfy the basic presentation goal. Do not spend
  the next pass selecting one universal relief scale.
- Dramatic at 250k strongly exposes fine-mesh facets and jagged summit geometry.
  Separate resolution/polygonal rendering effects from modeled slopes before
  changing terrain.
- Snow/hypsometric saturation can make summit surfaces appear even flatter;
  slope/hillshade diagnostics without categorical color are required.
- One shared close-up altitude does not frame small lakes, continent-scale
  ranges and 5,000-km rivers equally. Future packets should consume each
  dossier target's focus radius.
- Plate, pre/post surface, integration-cut, drainage and climate overlays are
  still missing from matched capture packets.

### Coasts and other missing semantics

- Coastline is currently a zero-elevation boundary after a target-land-fraction
  datum shift and hydrologic repair, not a coastal process or water-volume
  solution.
- There is no stable island/coast hierarchy or topology-aware generalization.
- Range/plateau/pass/valley/divide semantics remain provisional; water and
  river semantics are further along but incomplete.
- Persistent sediment/source-to-sink, storage/wetlands, cryosphere, soils,
  ecology, resources and human geography remain candidates. Revisit them after
  the current geographic-coherence disposition, not after every local finding.
