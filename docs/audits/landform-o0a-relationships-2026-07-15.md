## Result

O0a can now relate one exact planar G0 graph, its independently derived S0
surface hierarchy and its independently derived D0 drainage tree without
consuming a terrain arm's native answers or presentation state. It verifies the
predecessor payload hashes before deriving evidence and emits a separate
configuration namespace and evidence hash.

The retained evidence includes:

- a whole-domain validated planar subdivision and a certified regular-testbed or
  registered projected-R1 geometry identity;
- exact one-copy backing of every reference D0 raw owner seam to its physical
  reciprocal face;
- mutually exclusive `FlowTransition` and `LateralBoundaryCandidate` roles,
  with owner ancestry retained separately;
- memoized physical-surface receiver traces, signed descent margins and all D0
  conditioning provenance needed for bilateral-descent evidence;
- reference-highland boundary support and S0-saddle-to-boundary association;
- reference-reach longitudinal samples and nested-catchment cross sections with
  physical cell means, explicitly marked boundary-height proxies and censor
  reasons; and
- deterministic fixed-int little-endian serialization under an O0a-specific
  schema and hash.

This is a pass for an evaluation instrument. It is not a finding that its raw
faces are geomorphic divides, that bilateral descent identifies a ridge, or
that a positive cross section defines a valley.

## Corrections completed before verdict

Adversarial review found and corrected several issues before this result was
accepted:

- full-graph point and face scans were replaced by one deterministic spatial
  index, and candidate-face work is now counted rather than reported as zero;
- domain-boundary exits, axis-on-boundary precedence and forward supported
  collinearity are explicit;
- the complete registered sample lattice is retained, boundary-height proxies
  cannot contaminate physical-sample extrema, and missing spans have explicit
  reasons;
- longitudinal flags require positive segment overlap and failed point
  location is typed rather than repaired with a donor-cell fallback;
- newly derived zeroes and every numerical result family receive finite and
  canonical checks;
- regular geometry identity handles registered f32 operator quantization and
  portal-split collinear vertices while still certifying six true hex corners;
- projected-R1 identity is checked against the registered adapter output, not
  a caller's self-description; and
- ambiguous raw backing is detected before ordinary raw-multiset mismatch.

The manufactured matrix is therefore evidence for the corrected implementation
at `4467582`, not for the earlier intermediate state.
