# Hex3 model strategy

Status: **current project decision**, 2026-07-17.

Hex3 is an **authentic systemic world generator** built from reduced causal
models. It uses different fidelity levels by domain, but one common rule:
preserve the relationships that make geography intelligible, then present
those relationships cartographically for legibility and spectacle.

This is neither a claim to simulate an Earth system nor permission to assemble
an arbitrary game map. It is the project-level answer to what kind of model
Hex3 is trying to become.

## Decision

Adopt a hybrid-by-domain strategy:

> Generate geography through reduced causal models grounded in physical
> systems; derive stable, explainable geographic objects; present them through
> declared cartographic transformations optimized for legibility and wonder.

The world model owns causes and state. The semantic model owns geographic
meaning. The presentation model owns visual emphasis. Fidelity may differ
between domains, but these ownership boundaries do not.

The first coherence and disposition pass is complete enough to choose work. It
found a real mountain-organization defect but did not justify the tested deeper
terrain owners. The broader decision in the
[cross-system disposition](system-disposition.md) keeps the cheap causal spine,
parks that mountain branch, and selected **Water Geography V0** as the next
enabling slice. That slice has since passed its bounded exit gate; the next
expansion still requires a separate portfolio/design decision. This is not a
commitment to deepen every retained system.

## Alternatives considered

### Reduced physical planet

This is a useful discipline but an unsuitable global promise. Hex3 cannot
currently support predictive or quantitatively Earth-like claims across
tectonics, climate, erosion and hydrology. Treating increasing physical depth
as the universal direction would spend heavily on mechanisms whose visible or
downstream value is uncertain.

**Disposition:** use physical reduction where budgets, topology, scaling or
causal response matter; do not make whole-planet fidelity the product identity.

### Physically grounded mini-planet

A smaller fictional planet could make relief legible without cartographic
exaggeration, but it changes gravity, atmosphere, circulation, drainage and
characteristic scales together. It is a valid future configuration only if
those consequences are deliberately modeled.

**Disposition:** retain as a bounded experiment, not an invisible solution to
a rendering problem and not the current global default.

### Game-like world generator

Directly authoring categorical terrain can give strong board readability and
fast control, but it weakens causal explanation and risks making downstream
systems independent decorative layers.

**Disposition:** use game-like rules as disclosed authentic hacks or semantic
generalization, not as an untracked replacement for the causal substrate.

### One fidelity class everywhere

Uniform fidelity sounds clean but is economically and scientifically false.
Geometry, water topology, climate proxies and rendering have different useful
truths and different costs.

**Disposition:** reject. Architectural coherence comes from explicit contracts
and ownership, not identical equations in every domain.

## Domain truth contracts

| Domain | Truth promised | Current posture | Not promised |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sphere, tessellation and units | Numerically coherent geometry, adjacency, area, distance and declared conversions | Physical/numerical foundation | Geodetic reconstruction of a real body |
| Plates and motion | Coherent spherical plate ownership and Euler-pole kinematics | Physically grounded kinematic model | Predictive mantle convection or real plate histories |
| Tectonic terrain | Terrain responds in the right places and directions to crust and boundary relationships | Reduced causal model; legacy product remains the baseline while alternatives are judged | Quantitative lithosphere mechanics or geological reconstruction |
| Erosion and landform evolution | Drainage-related processes reorganize relief with controlled, explainable responses | Reduced landscape operator requiring object-level evaluation | Calibrated geological time, complete sediment transport or stratigraphy |
| Climate | Temperature, moisture and wind proxies respond coherently to latitude, terrain and circulation assumptions | Mechanism-informed authentic model | Weather prediction or a complete atmosphere–ocean energy budget |
| Hydrology | Water follows coherent topology; basins, outlets, lakes and rivers have shared identity | Physical topology with disclosed equilibrium/storage hacks | Event hydrology, calibrated discharge chronology or full groundwater |
| Geographic semantics | Reusable objects are derived from state, retain provenance and explain their causes | Required product layer; incomplete | Ontological truth independent of scale or purpose |
| Rendering | One world can be shown physically, diagnostically, cartographically and dramatically | Explicit visual/cartographic model; profile work incomplete | Rendered slope, width or visibility as physical evidence |
| Ecology, resources and civilization | Later systems consume geography and expose its consequences | Future semantic/systemic layers | Automatic priority merely because they complete a planet checklist |

These are current claims, not protection for current implementations. A system
may be simplified, replaced or removed if a cheaper mechanism preserves its
contract—or if the contract itself has little product value.

## What qualifies as an authentic hack

An authentic hack need not reproduce the hidden mechanism. It must preserve a
declared set of consequences that its consumers and viewers rely on. Depending
on the domain, those may include:

- topology and connectivity;
- sign and ordering of responses;
- characteristic location, orientation and scale;
- conservation or a bounded ledger;
- conditional relationships between cause and outcome;
- useful downstream consequences under controlled changes.

Matching a marginal distribution or producing an Earth-like screenshot is not
enough. Priors, clamps, remappings and distribution-forcing operations must be
disclosed. A hack becomes unjustified when it contradicts downstream state,
hides the owner of an effect, or adds complexity without visible, explanatory
or reusable benefit.

## Spectacle and cartography

Spectacle is a product goal and a test of communicative success. It is not
evidence that the world model is correct.

Relief exaggeration, widened rivers, selective visibility, color hierarchy,
lighting and generalization are legitimate ways to communicate planetary
structure. They must be recorded as presentation state and compared with
physical and diagnostic views. The earlier apparent “pillar” failure is the
canonical warning: presentation scale can amplify a reasonable modeled slope
into an apparently impossible object.

The project therefore does not resolve the mountain/rivers visibility conflict
by distorting physical state. It resolves it by retaining physical state,
deriving the feature worth showing, and applying an explicit presentation
transform.

## Strategic sequence

The completed first pass asked four questions in order:

1. **What geography exists?** Extract ranges, plateaus, passes, valleys,
   divides, basins, coasts and river hierarchy as scale-aware objects rather
   than loose thresholds or render primitives.
2. **Why does it exist?** Relate those objects to tectonic, erosional, climatic
   and hydrologic causes, including counterexamples and missing relationships.
3. **Can it be read?** Inspect matched Physical, Diagnostic, Cartographic and
   Dramatic views, with all exaggeration and generalization declared.
4. **Which mechanisms earn their place?** Give each current and proposed system
   a retain, simplify, replace, quarantine, remove or research disposition
   based on causal correspondence, visible payoff, downstream leverage and
   cost.

That pass produced explicit system dispositions rather than one universal
fidelity direction. The selected sequence is now:

1. repair and validate the shared climate–water–basin–river–coast truth contract
   using existing cheap systems;
2. if those inputs become coherent, build a bounded living surface as the first
   actual world expansion;
3. keep persistent source-to-sink sediment behind an explicit time, material,
   river-ownership and memory design gate; and
4. return to tectonic forcing only with real front topology, history and
   inherited material state—not another manufactured mountain rung.

Missing systems remain central, but continue to compete against fixing,
simplifying or deleting current systems.

## Gates against local optimization

Deeper simulation or parameter tuning is not roadmap work merely because a
metric can be improved. It must identify:

- the geographic object or relationship being improved;
- the claimed truth contract and present failure;
- the matched views and controlled response that would distinguish success;
- the visible or downstream benefit;
- the simpler alternative and compute/maintenance cost;
- a stopping rule if the added mechanism does not earn that cost.

Performance work remains appropriate for a demonstrated iteration bottleneck,
but a faster version of an undecided model is not strategic progress.

## Success criterion

Hex3 succeeds when a world is memorable at globe scale, legible as a map, and
more interesting under inspection because its features have coherent causes.
The system should generate geographic situations worth noticing—not merely
fields that pass isolated metrics—and should be honest about which parts are
physics, reduced mechanism, authored prior and visual interpretation.
