## Decision

Compare exactly three owners on the frozen linked case:

- **H:** an idealized planar hold-and-carve control;
- **C:** a reduced, dimensioned coevolving cell-mean landscape; and
- **G:** a disclosed graph-first reconstruction.

The question is not which arm makes the tallest, sharpest or least plateau-like
surface. It is which minimum owner creates useful regional highland/drainage
organization, communicates the actual linked forcing geometry, survives
numerical and causal controls, and justifies its compute and architecture cost.
Broad plateaus remain legitimate landforms. The defect under investigation is a
universal smooth capped grammar without differentiated peaks, saddles, divides,
drainage hierarchy and internal relief.

This design freezes the owner families, common input, opportunity philosophy,
candidate base algorithms and anti-circular evidence boundary. The subsequent
[artifact/provenance amendment](orogen-organization-artifact-v0-2026-07-16.md)
now freezes semantic arm bytes, and the
[numerical/admission amendment](orogen-organization-numerical-v0-2026-07-16.md)
freezes execution and validity, and the
[evidence/projection amendment](orogen-organization-evidence-v0-2026-07-16.md)
freezes common extraction, central/G projections, correspondence, discrepancy
and materiality evidence. The
[planar capture/human-review amendment](orogen-organization-planar-review-v0-2026-07-16.md)
now freezes deterministic presentation, observation order and reveal. This
design does not promote a product terrain path. No arm can
be chosen for the product from the linked base case alone.

This design supersedes the older competitive H/S/C/D/E matrix and its 29-run
program. Synthetic drainage-first construction is represented by G. An
explicit joint ridge/valley skeleton remains absent and may only return as a
separately registered noncompetitive upper bound; it is not hidden inside G.
Geological provinces, sediment, flexure, glaciers and explicit faults are not

modes. Its `3-`/`3+` checkpoints, requirement that every arm claim 10 Myr, old

thin call into product `ErosionState`, and a passing H result does not validate
the product's physical chronology.

Physical state, semantic evidence and presentation are separate owners. The

## Common terrain opportunity

Let `z0[i]` be stored initial elevation, `D[i]` stored cumulative rock
displacement and `A[i]` cell area. `V` is the exact predecessor declaration
`analytic_rock_volume_km3`, not a resolution reduction. The discrete
`sum(D[i] A[i])` is separately measured in stored order and its residual from
`V` remains evidence. The registered opportunity authority is:

```text
V = 100625 km3
z_opportunity[i] = z0[i] + D[i]
```

This is a calibration authority, not a shared pre-arm terrain and not scored as
an arm. H and C opportunity controls disable incision and hillslope transport.
G has no separable active process after reconstruction, so its defining graph
and reconstruction are necessarily its calibration and base result; that result
is frozen without human inspection and revealed only with the other arms.

- H's target-only final surface must equal `z_opportunity` bit-for-bit.
- C uplift-only must reproduce `z_opportunity` within
  `1e-6 km3 + 5e-7 * V` in area-integrated volume and within `2e-5 km` per
  cell. This deliberately covers the registered f32 frame-integration and
  midpoint-quadrature boundary without pretending it is a bitwise f64 rebuild;
  the numerical amendment requires a f64 manufactured oracle before it becomes
  an accepted predecessor.
- G builds its frozen forest/reconstruction and determines its sole dimensioned
  amplitude `a_G` at **4 km only** so its positive elevation-volume addition is
  `V`. The solve tolerance is `1e-8 km3 + 5e-12 * V`. The same
  `a_G` bits are then used at 8 and 2 km and retained in later wet/dry response
  runs. The numerical amendment freezes the monotone solve and analytic gates.
  Finite 8/2 km work drift is scale-transfer evidence, not a numerical failure;
  no result-dependent band or recalibration hides it.

Only total positive opportunity volume at the reference 4 km calibration is
matched for G. G's spatial allocation is an explicit architecture degree of
freedom, so this is a **work-matched**, not broad-envelope-matched, comparison.
Centroid, covariance, footprint, peak, cap, slope, drainage and visual relief
are reported but never matched. H and C have scale one. No final arm result is
rescaled, shifted to equal a peak, quantile-normalized, relief-normalized or
volume-normalized after its active processes run.

The opportunity audit records total volume, area-weighted centroid and
covariance of positive added opportunity for every arm. Reference solve error
has a numerical gate; cross-resolution differences are evidence, not tuning
instructions.

## Shared active-process regime

H and C use the same minimal carve laws so their primary difference is regional
ownership rather than process inventory:

```text
effective areal denudation E = K q^m S^n
K = 1.0e-4 km^-1
m = 1
n = 1
q = unfiltered C0 physical specific discharge, km2/Myr

linear hillslope diffusivity D_h = 0.1 km2/Myr
runoff = exact stored local supply
```

The hillslope implementation must add an explicit conservative **linear** mode.
The existing nonlinear critical-slope law is not the base comparison and may
not be approximated by silently choosing a very large threshold. Internal face
flux remains antisymmetric; portal faces use the registered linear Dirichlet
boundary flux. H and C use no Q16 Helmholtz support, C1 channel state,
deposition, lake integration, lithology, climate feedback, drainage pulse,
A4 redistribution, glaciers or product repair.

Both use the current C0 accuracy controls:

```text
maximum uplift depth        = 0.02 km       # C only
maximum denudation depth    = 0.02 km
denudation slope Courant    = 0.25
minimum accepted dt         = 1e-8 Myr
maximum adaptive attempts   = 16
hillslope timestep safety   = 0.4
requested maximum dt        = 0.01 Myr
```

These are numerical controls, not morphology knobs. The provisional K regime
is frozen before linked results and retains its truth limit: C0 represents
effective areal lowering of a cell mean, not a resolved channel bed, width,
gorge, sediment body, lateral erosion law or valley cross-section.

The regime is not an Earth calibration. It is the already-frozen dimensional
control `E0/(q0*S0)` with `E0=0.1 km/Myr`, `q0=50,000 km2/Myr` and `S0=0.02`,
and its corrected 0.1 Myr U/L screen has convergent integrated relief and closed
ledgers at 8/4/2. Extending it to 10 Myr is deliberately a falsification of this
minimal owner, not permission to retune K. The numerical amendment requires the
short-run analytic/regression gates before the long run and halts on minimum-dt
or adaptive-attempt exhaustion.

## H: idealized hold-and-carve control

H owns physical elevation and a calibrated pass counter. It makes no geological
time claim. It uses 200 declared passes to preserve the architecture of a
reasserted smooth opportunity envelope without importing product units or
product-only systems.

Define normalized pass progress `u_k = k/200` for `k=0..200`. Map it to the
linked schedule coordinate `t_k = 10 u_k Myr` only to obtain the cumulative
activity fraction:

```text
p_k = integral_0^t_k activity(t) dt / 5.75
target_k[i] = z0[i] + p_k D[i]
```

The `Myr` in this mapping is an input-schedule coordinate and operator exposure,
not H's terrain age. At each pass `k=1..200`:

1. apply the hold rule `z[i] = max(z[i], target_k[i])`;
2. ledger the exact positive volume introduced by that rule;
3. evolve zero-uplift C0 denudation and linear hillslopes for exactly
   `0.05 operator-Myr` **in this pass**, using adaptive substeps no larger than
   `0.01 Myr` (10 operator-Myr total over 200 passes); and
4. rebuild water routing from the current surface during those accepted
   substeps without writing routing elevation into physical elevation.

The final pass ends after carving; it does not re-pin the target. H reports
separately the original opportunity `V`, cumulative hold restoration, fluvial
export, hillslope portal transfer and final elevation-volume moment. Hold
restoration beyond `V` is not hidden or treated as C rock work.

H has no product deposition, lake bases, crust-thickness/Airy state, repeated
`0.003` product uplift, emergent-envelope mode or A4 trunk redistribution. It
is a bounded control, not a claim to reproduce the complete product path.

Native H checkpoints are passes `[0, 50, 120, 200]`. They are provenance, not
cross-arm temporal correspondence.

The numerical amendment now provides the analytic cumulative-smoothstep
primitive used for `p_k`, sets `p_0=0` and `p_200=1` by exact endpoint rule, and
defines the 400-pass sensitivity as `u_k=k/400` with `0.025 operator-Myr` per
pass and the same 10 operator-Myr total.

## C: reduced coevolving owner

C is the transactional C0 cell-mean owner with the frozen linear-hillslope
amendment. It alone claims physical time. It starts from stored `z0`, evaluates
the stored linked scenario/compiler at every accepted candidate midpoint and
evolves from 0 to 10 Myr:

```text
0--6 Myr   time-varying linked rock uplift plus denudation and hillslopes
6--10 Myr  zero forcing, continued drainage/denudation/hillslope relaxation
```

Each adaptive retry resamples forcing at its candidate midpoint. Drainage is
derived from the current physical surface and exact stored local supply.
Priority/depression routing may derive a virtual routing surface but cannot
mutate bedrock. Portal levels remain boundary-face conditions.

C retains physical time, revision, f64 cell-mean bedrock, water diagnostics,
operator limits and the elevation-volume-moment ledger. It does not retain
persistent channel/reach identity. Native flow fields are provenance and may
not enter common scoring.

Native C full-surface checkpoints are `[0, 3, 6, 10] Myr`; scalar/operator
diagnostics are recorded at every accepted step. The common comparison uses
only initial and final authoritative surfaces.

## G: graph-first authentic control

G has no geological chronology. It builds one deterministic portal-rooted
receiver forest from a nonauthoritative planning field, reconstructs one
physical surface and discloses the authored topology.

### Construction forest

Use the planning scalar:

```text
p[i] = z0[i] + D[i]
```

Run a multi-source minimax priority flood from every open portal face. Portal
base levels are the external roots; cells remain unpinned. The state for a
candidate path to cell `i` is the maximum planning value encountered on that
path. The queue key is exactly:

```text
(path_maximum ordered by f64::total_cmp, portal_id, cell_index,
 receiver_kind, receiver_index)
```

For a portal-owner seed, the candidate maximum is `max(base_level, p[i])` and
`receiver` is the portal. For an internal candidate from finalized cell `j` to
neighbor `i`, it is `max(finalized_max[j], p[i])` and receiver `j`. Standard
Dijkstra finalization is used: a cell's receiver is frozen only when its best
key is popped. Ties follow the full key above. CSR neighbor order is evidence
but not the tie-break authority.

The receiver always points to a previously finalized cell, so the result is an
acyclic whole-domain forest terminating at portal 0 or 1. Accumulate exact
stored local runoff and physical cell area downstream. Derive Strahler order
and the registered 1,000/2,000/4,000 km2 support summaries for provenance only;
the complete forest, not a thresholded channel subset, owns reconstruction.

G construction has its own schema/domain and must not call common D0 assembly,
read a common D0 result or inspect H/C/common object answers. A shared low-level
priority-queue helper is permissible only if construction and evidence retain
separate configurations, hashes and entry points.

The minimax forest is an authentic structural prior: it chooses outlet paths
that cross the lowest available planning barriers while preserving exact portal
connectivity. It is not claimed to simulate channel initiation, incision or
divide migration.

### Surface reconstruction

Accumulate runoff/area in reverse finalization order (upstream to downstream).
Let `Q[i]` be accumulated stored runoff and `ell[i]` the center-to-receiver
distance. For a portal receiver, `ell[i]` is the minimum physical
center-to-open-face distance for the selected portal. Define the nonnegative
raw edge term:

```text
Q_ref = 500000 km3/Myr
w[i] = D[i] * ell[i] / sqrt(Q[i] / Q_ref)      # km2
```

All quantities must be finite; stored positive local runoff guarantees
`Q[i] > 0`. For a proposed `a >= 0` with units `km^-1`, reconstruct in
finalization order (downstream to upstream):

```text
portal receiver:
  z_a[i] = max(z0[i], next_up(portal_base_level)) + a * w[i]

internal receiver j:
  z_a[i] = max(z0[i], next_up(z_a[j])) + a * w[i]
```

`next_up(x)` means the least finite f64 strictly greater than `x`. Therefore
every authored internal receiver is strictly downhill on the emitted surface,
and every portal owner is strictly above its external base level, including
where `D[i]` is zero. If this invariant fails under fresh reconstruction, G is
invalid rather than merely a poor morphological result.

At 4 km solve the monotone equation
`sum(max(z_a[i] - z0[i], 0) * A[i]) = V` for `a_G`, using the exact bracket,
iteration and termination rule frozen by the numerical amendment. A zero or
non-finite denominator/solution rejects. Reuse the exact 4 km `a_G` at 8/2 km;
do not absorb resolution drift by recalibration.

`Q_ref` only fixes raw units and is absorbed by reference amplitude calibration;
it is retained so later wet/dry runs use the same `a_G` rather than being
renormalized. G performs no post-reconstruction erosion, smoothing, noise,
incision or target-object fitting in V0.

The runoff-conditioned upstream rise is a disclosed steady-state
stream-power-like reconstruction hack, not a time integration or equilibrium
claim. Its value is whether this cheap causal compression produces useful
independent objects, not whether its raw profile equation is deeper physics.

The authored forest, accumulated area/runoff, Strahler/support summaries,
planning field hash, raw-relief hash and `a_G` are native provenance. They are
never substituted for independent D0 evidence. Direct authored-versus-extracted
receiver/portal/support correspondence is reported as architecture evidence.

G native checkpoints are typed construction stages `Input`, `Forest` and
`Reconstruction`; they are not time.

## Evaluation population: whole context, central projection

Do not add false cells to V0 `scored_cell` or reinterpret
`WholeGraphSupportV0`. For each final arm surface:

1. adapt the complete stored Voronoi mesh to the accepted planar control-volume
   graph;
2. assemble the accepted common core with `scored_cell = true` for every cell,

3. build reference O0a over that whole core; and
4. derive a separately versioned central projection bound to the stored

Whole-domain extraction supplies routing and topological context. The central
window is not a hard geomorphic boundary, so no feature, footprint, catchment or
reach is clipped at `abs(x)=320` or `abs(y)=160`.

## Mechanical linked-case probes

The evidence layer adds no named natural kinds. It reports:

1. for each central-anchored reference highland, the area-integral of each
   stored segment support stencil over its whole footprint, footprint centroid
   distance to each segment polyline and orientation difference where defined;
2. central cell and object distributions for physical elevation, 25/50/100 km
   relief, grade, persistence, footprint, anisotropy and 0.25/0.5/1 km summit
   caps, with any quantity not already public in S0 added by the versioned
   evidence amendment rather than inferred from private implementation state;
3. D0 outlet, basin, reach, catchment, Strahler, trunk and central-supply
   evidence using the accepted whole-context derivation;
4. O0a backed-face, descent, saddle-boundary and cross-section evidence without
   renaming those records ridges, passes or valleys;
5. direct G-native versus independent-D0 receiver, portal, accumulated-area and
   support correspondence through a new versioned instrument whose exact
   schema and reductions are frozen before it can support a verdict; and
6. the following mechanical transfer-zone probe.

The transfer probe is the bounding box of segment 0's terminal point and
segment 1's initial point, expanded by half the declared 100 km segment width:

```text
x in [-120, 40] km
y in [ -72, 72] km
```

Report its area-weighted elevation/grade/relief summaries, every accepted S0
saddle with backing cells inside it, every D0 reach/catchment contribution
inside it and the two forcing-stencil integrals. Do not select a privileged
"lowest" saddle or narrate an unspecified reach as passing "through" the box.
The evidence amendment must freeze backing-cell membership, boundary contact,
portal-owned contributions, ordering and null cases. Call it only
`TransferProbeV0`. A low or saddle there is response to offset tapered scalar
opportunity, not proof of a tectonic transfer fault.

Keep the evidence vector. Do not collapse it into a quality score or treat ten
O0a sensitivities as ten votes. Reference O0a is mandatory. Any claim that will
depend on a non-reference O0a choice must name that dependence in the executable
evidence amendment and run the complete accepted sensitivity suite; the suite
may not be added after seeing which arm it favors.

## Correspondence and observed discrepancy

For each arm, build whole-core O0b in both directions for 4↔8 and 4↔2. At 4 km,
also build both directions for H↔C, H↔G and C↔G. Same-mesh physical arrays are
compared directly without resampling.

O0b remains mechanical correspondence, not persistent identity or capture
truth. Report all positive/best partners, ties, coverage, overlap and
displacement. A typed D0/O0 failure is an instrument halt for that comparison,
not automatically evidence that the arm made bad terrain.

O0b is many-to-many and can contain ties, splits and merges. There is therefore
no generic scalar "uncertainty" obtained by subtracting two object values. The
comparison reports two distinct discrepancy sources: cross-resolution O0b
correspondence evidence and same-resolution numerical sensitivity. The
numerical amendment freezes direct same-cell H/C discrepancy reductions. The
evidence/projection amendment owns quantity-by-quantity object populations,
correspondence, missing partners, ties and compatibility predicates. Quantities
without a registered reduction remain descriptive and cannot support a
materiality claim.

C's numerical sensitivity halves requested maximum dt from 0.01 to 0.005 Myr
at 4 km. H doubles passes from 200 to 400 while preserving total target
progress and calibrated process exposure. G has no iterative timestep/pass
sensitivity; its same-process deterministic repeat must be exact. These
sensitivity runs are numerical controls, not alternative arms.

Do not require every count to change monotonically at 8/4/2. A claimed causal
or arm difference must exceed its registered quantity-specific observed-
discrepancy rule and retain compatible principal objects. Resolution
instability is reported and blocks a conclusion that depends on it; it is not
repaired by threshold tuning.

The accepted shared bundle has one lattice orientation. V0 therefore makes no
rotation-invariance claim. A rotated-mesh control requires a separately
preregistered input identity and cannot mutate this bundle after results. No
arm may be promoted from this program until that separate rotated-input control
has been executed or the promotion claim explicitly excludes orientation
robustness and explains why that exclusion is fit for product use.

`artifacts/orogen-owner-v0/comparison/`. Targets must not exist; publication
uses validated temporary siblings and records revision/dirty state, executable,
toolchain, OS/CPU, thread count, command, wall time, peak RSS authority and file
hashes. JSON is a projection; binary is semantic authority.

Standalone decoders use bounded allocation, reject trailing bytes and validate
canonical values, array shapes/order, local component hashes and declared
predecessor identities. Full predecessor validation additionally receives the
exact input, native provenance, arm result and evidence predecessors needed to
reconstruct and compare semantic content. It establishes exact bindings and
local consistency, not solver causation: a mutually repaired changed final
array can still be internally and predecessor-consistent. Only deterministic
replay establishes production by the declared algorithm. The artifact
amendment defines these three boundaries and their failure types rather than
implying an outer checksum proves causal provenance.

## Resource and complexity accounting

Release compilation and shared-input materialization are excluded. Input load,
validation, arm generation, semantic output validation and atomic publication
are included in each arm run. Evidence extraction is measured separately.

Per arm/resolution ceilings on the development WSL machine are:

| spacing | arm generation wall time | peak RSS | retained result directory |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 km | 30 minutes | 2 GiB | 2 GiB |
| 4 km | 90 minutes | 2 GiB | 2 GiB |
| 2 km | 6 hours | 2 GiB | 2 GiB |

These are hard safety ceilings, not expected runtimes or evidence that an arm is
Pareto-competitive. Their loose 2 km wall-time allowance is grounded by the
[corrected C0 support audit](../audits/c0-support-discriminator-2026-07-13.md),
where 0.1 Myr C0-V U/L runs took approximately 162/114 seconds at 2 km; a
10 Myr adaptive coevolution can plausibly enter multi-hour territory. Actual
time, memory, artifact size and reusable outputs remain comparison evidence,
and a design that merely fits below the ceiling is not thereby justified.

Each evidence directory has a 15-minute, 2 GiB ceiling after release
compilation. Pairwise comparison has a 60-minute, 2 GiB ceiling. A ceiling
breach stops for architecture review; it does not license skipped validation,
coarser hidden settings or a larger budget after seeing morphology. Cheap arms
are not required to spend the allowance.

The deterministic reference uses one materializer/solver thread. Record
available parallelism. Separately report:

- production lines and modules added per arm, excluding tests/generated code;
- number of frozen morphology/graph constants and seeded objects;
- dynamic bytes per cell and retained native graph bytes;
- asymptotic dominant work and accepted steps/passes/queue operations;
- wall time, peak RSS and artifact bytes; and
- which downstream reusable state exists beyond the final surface.

These are architecture evidence, not a scalar penalty formula.

## Matched presentation and human-review design

Numeric evidence is authoritative on Linux. A generic planar capture adapter
must consume only the stored mesh, frozen arm result and independent evidence.
It cannot call product `World`, inspect native arm graphs for the common overlay
or choose a camera from an arm's highest point.

The bounds, layers and primary ranges below are the minimum logical capture
design. The executable
[planar capture/human-review amendment](orogen-organization-planar-review-v0-2026-07-16.md)
now makes them reproducible by freezing the CPU renderer/build boundary,
rasterization and sampling, view matrices, lighting, water/overlay ordering,
bound UTF-8 sidecar legends, palettes, PNG encoding, fallback ladder, procedural permutation,
primary-before-resolution forms and reveal records.

Freeze north-up orthographic plan views:

| view | bounds km | raster |
|---|---|---:|
| full domain | stored full control-volume union | 1440 x 960 |
| central | `[-320,320] x [-160,160]` | 1440 x 720 |
| transfer probe | `[-120,40] x [-72,72]` | 1200 x 1080 |

Every arm uses identical bounds, projection, lighting, background, water,
legend and overlay order. Candidate mandatory layers are:

1. fixed-range physical elevation map, `[-0.25, 4.0] km`, with saturation
   counts reported;
2. Physical 1x geometry/hillshade;
3. fixed slope map, grade `[0,0.10]`, without snow or categorical biome color;
4. forcing support plus independent S0/D0/O0 overlay;
5. fixed Authentic cartographic geometry at the existing approximately 25x
   relief preset; and
6. a separate G-authored-graph provenance sheet, visually distinguished from
   the independent D0 overlay and never placed in the procedurally masked common
   sheet.

Primary evidence never uses per-arm quantile colors, per-arm vertical scaling,
arm-selected river thresholds or relief normalization. A supplementary range
may be added only after retaining the fixed-range image and declaring why it
was saturated.

The first human comparison uses 4 km central/full/transfer sheets with arm
labels replaced by A/B/C. Tile order is a deterministic permutation from the
literal seed `orogen-owner-review-order-v0`, independent of result bytes. The
key is stored separately until observations are written. Review prompts ask
about differentiated highlands, internal crest/saddle/drainage organization,
forcing communication, obvious faceting/artifacts and overall usefulness. They
do not ask which image is “most realistic” or disclose which arm is physical.

Physical will look flat and remains diagnostic truth. Authentic is legitimate
cartographic evidence but cannot establish physical success. Human review is
retained alongside, not substituted for, mechanical and numerical evidence.

## Later response protocols

The base audit may recommend which valid arms advance, but cannot promote one.
Before response execution, publish exact shared-input identities for the cases.

Wet/dry uses the same L geometry/work/material and exact runoff multipliers
`2.0` and `0.5`. H repeats its fixed pass/hold rule with the changed stored
runoff; C evolves the full 0--10 Myr physical run; G rebuilds its forest and raw
relief with changed accumulated runoff while retaining the reference-L `a_G`.
No arm receives a post-response amplitude calibration.

Because V0 multiplies spatially uniform runoff and G's forest planning field is
independent of runoff, G must reproduce the identical authored forest in the
wet and dry cases. Only accumulated discharge and the reconstructed profile
scale respond. This is a deliberately limited runoff-response control, not
evidence that G models divide migration or topology response.

Forcing reorganization requires a new input bundle containing the complete
time-dependent shifted forcing and its cumulative counterfactual. H updates its
target through the registered hold schedule without resetting terrain; C
evolves continuously through the transition; G emits frozen pre/post forests,
reconstructs once under the new opportunity and reports graph correspondence.
G makes no claim that the reconstruction jump is geological time. H remains the
standing control in every response case.

The exact R/R0 geometry, snapshots and hashes are not invented here. They must
be committed before implementation of that case. A base result cannot be used
to choose them.

## Decision rule and stop condition

The base L audit reports the valid Pareto set; it does not pick a product owner.
It reports each preregistered claim against its own registered numerical and
resolution discrepancy rule and retains the complete evidence vector:

- S0 highland/saddle/footprint organization;
- D0 basin/reach/trunk/central-supply organization;
- forcing/highland/transfer mechanical correspondence;
- physical multiscale relief, grade and summit-cap morphology;
- procedurally masked matched human judgment; and
- downstream reusable state at justified compute/complexity cost.

These families are correlated and carry no vote count. A conclusion must state
which causal/product claim is supported, which evidence is genuinely distinct,
which evidence conflicts and why the combination is sufficient. No `k`-of-`n`
rule or hidden scalar quality score is allowed.

Prefer C over a cheaper G only when C's dynamics or outputs provide material
object-level, response or downstream value commensurate with incremental cost.
Prefer G or a later C/G hybrid when it preserves the needed causes and semantics
more cheaply. Retain H if alternatives do not justify replacement. Physical
depth is not a tie-breaker.

No arm is promoted until it also passes separately preregistered wet/dry and
reorganization/R0 controls and is preferred in matched human review. If C fails
while G succeeds, diagnose which missing physical consequence matters before
tuning. If G's native forest materially disagrees with independent drainage,
the authentic hack fails its own correspondence claim. If every arm is valid
but object-poor, revisit the comparison representation or missing shared state;
do not tune relief scale. If common evidence fails across arms, repair the
instrument rather than terrain.

The artifact/provenance, numerical/admission, evidence/projection and planar
capture/human-review amendments are now committed in design. Implementation
starts with the pre-arm renderer checkpoint and shared composition/artifact
infrastructure, followed by the frozen base campaign. The next task is not a
new ecology, sediment, tectonic or product model.
