# Orogen dissection causal transplant — 2026-07-20

Status: recovered historical positive control and current-product causal
comparison. This note changes the interpretation of old O0 evidence; it does
not promote O0, change a product default or reopen terrain work ahead of the
roadmap gate.

## Decision

Retain the **dependency graph**, not the historical implementation:

```text
organized and heterogeneous convergent uplift opportunity
  -> evolving runoff routing and headwater-eligible incision
  -> valley lowering plus hillslope response
  -> subordinate valleys, divides and branch ridges
  -> physical terrain
  -> declared cartographic presentation
```

The recovered O0 comparison shows that this interaction can create organized
fine morphology worth resolving. It does not show that erosion will organize
an arbitrary smooth mountain, that the old O0 organizer was geologically
authentic, or that a million global cells is the correct product budget.

The current product retains much of the downstream response but has removed
the organized input. It interpolates a broad legacy distance-band roof and
then reapplies essentially the same scalar tectonic footprint during 200
hold-and-carve steps. Routing can dissect that roof, but it is not supplied the
regional highs, ends, lows or cross-belt differences from which to elaborate a
convincing range hierarchy.

## What the recovered control establishes

The exact adjacent historical source comparison is `4add9eb` to `a9bc33f`. Its
only source change lowered the global channel-support default from 30 to 4 km²;
O0 itself remained default-off at those revisions. The reconstruction applies
30 and 4 explicitly under the same selected O0 stack. At about 951,000 fine
cells, lower support extends incision eligibility higher into the massif,
lowers and separates parts of it and leaves organized branching flank relief.
Much of that structure is lost at about 245,000 cells. It remains visible at
Authentic relief `0.04`, although support 4 is not uniformly more readable than
support 30 under the old renderer.

The adjacent pair isolates channel-support sensitivity **conditional on fixed
O0 organization**. It is not an organized-versus-uniform uplift ablation. The
attribution of the supplied first-order organization to O0 comes from its
implementation and surface ancestry, supported by the broader physical prior;
the pair directly establishes how the response changes when channel support
changes.

Together with the earlier O0 construction and ancestry evidence, this is
evidence for an interaction:

- front-organized, asymmetric and stochastically along-strike-varying uplift
  supplied first-order structure;
- routing plus stream-power incision extended valleys into that structure;
- fixed hillslope response participated in shaping the interfluves; and
- native fine cells retained generated morphology. A separate current-product
  display-only control showed that adding triangles to an unchanged coarse
  physical surface does not recreate native-fine morphology.

The packet is visual morphology evidence. It did not independently extract and
compare dendritic topology or watershed divides, and the fixed hillslope law
was not ablated. Historical lithology, structural-grain, deposition and
orographic-feedback mechanisms also remained active. They are constant across
the adjacent 30/4 comparison and therefore do not explain that delta, but their
contribution to the overall O0 morphology was never isolated. The captures
also include the selected major-river overlay, so their visible branching is
not a pure elevation-only discriminator.

It is not evidence that `4 km²` is a resolved or calibrated channel-head area.
It is sub-cell at about 245,000 cells and at best of order one finest cell at
about 951,000 cells, not a robustly multi-cell process scale. Nor is it evidence
that historical relief `0.2`, roughly 127 times physical radial scale, reported
realistic slopes. Authentic `0.04` is still about 25.5 times physical radial
scale.

The physical prior supports the same bounded statement. Finite fault or fold
systems, inherited structures, changing motion and material contrasts can
supply range ends, uplift maxima, unequal flanks and transfer lows. Incision
and hillslope transport can then generate valley spacing and fine branching,
move divides and amplify supplied differences. They do not guarantee
first-order massif segmentation from a uniform ribbon. The negative planar C
and finite-parent F responses are the relevant counterexamples.

## What not to transplant

Do not restore O0's implementation or constants wholesale:

- its Perlin along-front modulation is synthetic segmentation, not retained
  tectonic history;
- its chain coordinates use an arbitrary BFS seed, mishandle path arms/cycles,
  and do not explicitly preserve plate pair or polarity; collision also uses a
  shortcut side;
- demote/rebuild gain, global volume servo and land-floor behavior are bounded
  production hacks with datum and coast consequences;
- the 64/320 km cross-front widths, `K`, `m`, `n`, 200 steps, critical slope and
  4 km² support are calibrated closures rather than a physical clock or
  universal material law; and
- the exaggerated historical view is morphology evidence, not a slope target.

Randomness is not categorically forbidden. It is legitimate when it generates
unobserved **upstream history**—for example coherent terranes or inherited
structures before terrain exists—and when that state has independent semantics
or another consumer. Random samples chosen after observing a target to place
mountain highs, passes or grooves remain visual construction disguised as
geology. The current inheritance seam satisfies the first ownership rule in
form, but supplies no named structure on the reviewed collision parent and
therefore cannot justify subdividing that belt.

## Resolution and cheaper representations

The positive control rejects two simple claims: that resolution has no terrain
value, and that more display triangles can buy the value back. It does not
justify uniformly fine global simulation.

The Pareto question is which representation preserves the mutually compatible
tributary, valley-head, basin, divide and branch-ridge hierarchy:

1. selective physical refinement around causally predicted responsive terrain;
2. a coarse cell-mean surface plus persistent sub-grid drainage/divide state;
3. hierarchical uplift primitives followed by bounded local physical response;
4. a disclosed drainage-first authentic hack constrained by uplift, runoff,
   outlets and material; or
5. native fine simulation only where the cheaper representations fail.

Generic ridged noise, display subdivision and stencils around already-selected
major rivers are dominated: they do not own the missing subordinate graph.
The existing pilot also shows that relief responsiveness alone poorly predicts
channel heads and junctions, so adaptive refinement needs its own causal
predictor or a stable sub-grid state.

## Why regular drainage teeth can still be physical

A repeated valley/interfluve spacing is not by itself proof of a numerical or
visual artifact. Reduced landscape-evolution models with uniform uplift,
stream-power incision and hillslope transport select a characteristic drainage
wavelength through basin competition; spacing can become more regular as the
surface approaches equilibrium
([Perron et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1029/2007JF000977),
[Perron et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08174)). Reality broadens and
interrupts that grammar through inherited drainage, heterogeneous structure and
erodibility, rainfall gradients, capture, base-level change, sediment coupling
and lateral channel erosion. Facet experiments in particular find that
persistent drainage produces long, irregular multiangular forms rather than a
universal identical triangular train
([Hergarten 2024](https://doi.org/10.5194/esurf-12-1315-2024)).

This prior changes the interpretation, not the admission bar. Smooth forcing
followed by regular final outlets would identify a potentially authentic but
overexposed equilibrium response, not automatically a broken solver. Cadence
already phase-aligned with edge-rate forcing instead belongs upstream. Current
seed-`8675309` evidence takes the latter branch: owner convergence and final
crest covary strongly even in adjacent-station differences. The next question is
whether that input variation is meaningful curved-boundary kinematics or a
coarse boundary-orientation alias. A global smoother would erase both and cannot
answer it.

The follow-up identifies a narrower numerical/physical prior. A signed normal
velocity on one Voronoi face is legitimate as a control-volume flux; mapping
`max(face_flux, 0)` directly into terrain is not. Orientation errors which would
cancel in a finite-volume balance are rectified into positive uplift first.
Hex3 episode `9` shows exactly that signature: positive-per-edge opportunity is
22% above the signed chain total, while signed aggregation over the declared
collision support removes most of the excess without changing the signed
ledger. Exact edges should therefore continue to own topology and ledgers, but
compression should be classified after conservative physical-scale aggregation.

This matches continuum treatments of diffuse plate-boundary deformation, which
estimate velocity gradients over finite zones rather than interpreting every
polygon face as a separate uplift element
([Kreemer et al. 2003](https://geodesy.unr.edu/publications/Kreemer_et_al_GSRM_2003.pdf)).
It also leaves trench/strike-parallel motion distinct from normal thickening;
oblique convergence is commonly partitioned between thrusting and strike-slip
motion ([McCaffrey 1992](https://doi.org/10.1029/92JB00483)). A physical support
must be selected by mesh/phase stability and deformation width, not by which
render looks smoother.

## Consequence for the next terrain rung

The current Structural Mountain pause remains correct. Its source compiler is
more principled than O0's chaining, but the fixed generated parent contains no
defensible internal segmentation and the experimental inheritance generator
does not change that fact. The positive control therefore strengthens the
selected architecture without authorizing fabricated segments.

If the ordinary-world terrain escalation gate is met, the first implementation
should be a single-owner discriminator, not a parameter sweep:

1. generate or select organization entirely before terrain and state its
   provenance;
2. compile one budget-closed finite uplift opportunity;
3. remove both legacy convergent direct height and repeated convergent uplift
   from the candidate domain;
4. route and erode on one evolving surface, retaining the current low-support
   response as a control rather than a truth constant;
5. compare linear and bounded nonlinear hillslope response only as a causal
   ablation;
6. judge Physical topology, Authentic readability, basin/divide consequence,
   cost and survival under a coarser or sub-grid representation; and
7. stop if the result is another smooth massif, disconnected beads, or detail
   visible only through relief exaggeration.
